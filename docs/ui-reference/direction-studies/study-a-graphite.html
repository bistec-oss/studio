Direction study A · Draft review page

Graphite

Soft structuralism. Warm graphite neutrals, an ink accent that is pure tone, and floating opaque islands with diffused ambient shadows. The craft is in the bezels, the spacing and the motion, so a team's brand kit is the only colour on screen.

Palette

Warm-neutral greys (hue ≈ 45°, chroma under 4%). Accent = ink: near-black in light, bone in dark. Status hues are the only chroma in the UI.

Surface & elevation

  • Islands: header and sidebar float, inset 12 px, opaque surface-2/surface-1.
  • Double bezel: cards sit in a line-subtle tray (6 px) with a concentric inner radius.
  • Three shadows — panel, raised (menus, FAB), overlay (toasts, modals). All alpha-1 backgrounds, no backdrop-filter.

Density & spacing

Comfortable. 4 px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40. Controls 38 px (32 small). Body 14 px / 1.5.

Radius

8 · 12 · 18 · 26 px + pill. Buttons and inputs are pills; cards 26 px outer, 20 px inner.

Motion

Weighted spring curve cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1); 140 / 220 / 380 ms. Press = scale .98; menus pop from their anchor. Reduced motion: 150 ms opacity only.

Focus

2 px --focus outline, 2 px offset (shown on the refine input). Ink on canvas: ≥ 15:1 light, ≥ 13:1 dark.
