011 · Three directions for the draft review page

Pick one. Each study carries the exact :root / .dark token block the foundation (T4) will copy.

AGraphite

Open full size ↗

Soft structuralism. Graphite keeps today's comfortable rhythm and two-column page, but makes depth honest: opaque floating islands with soft ambient shadows replace frosted glass, and double-bezel trays give the cards a machined, premium feel without using any colour. The accent is ink — near-black in light, bone in dark — so the only chroma on screen is status and the team's own brand kit.

Trade-offs

  • + Closest to the current layout and density, so the screen restyles carry the least risk.
  • + A monochrome accent can never clash with any team's brand kit.
  • − A black primary button signposts less than a coloured one; hierarchy leans on weight, shape and the button-in-button nub.
  • − Bezels and pills cost space; dense admin tables will need a flatter variant.
  • − Soft shadows need separate tuning in dark mode.

BFolio

Open full size ↗

Editorial and flat. Folio treats the app as a publication desk: a paper ground, serif headlines, rules instead of boxes, and the post shown as a proof on a mat with crop marks, next to a numbered copy desk (caption, refine). It has the most personality of the three and suits a marketing team's craft. Its flat surface model is the simplest to keep opaque and fast.

Trade-offs

  • + Most distinctive, and the most readable for long text such as captions and briefings.
  • + No blur shadows to tune: floating surfaces are a border plus a hard offset.
  • − A strong taste. The warm cream ground slightly shifts how post colours read next to it.
  • − The sienna accent is a hue, so a warm or orange brand kit sits close to it.
  • − The nav is text-only, which scans worse as the sidebar grows, and there are three fonts to self-host.

CInstrument

Open full size ↗

A dense, tonal workbench. Instrument reframes the page as a pro tool. The post sits on a neutral dotted stage with size readouts, versions run along a filmstrip, and the caption and refine live in a right-hand inspector under one toolbar. True-neutral greys don't tint the post. Elevation is tonal plus hairlines, and the type is Geist with a mono "machine layer".

Trade-offs

  • + True neutrals make brand colours look most accurate; it has the highest density, which scales to queues and admin tables.
  • + The inspector pattern fits 012's per-channel captions and 008's model pickers.
  • − The biggest layout change (three panes), so the draft-page task is larger and riskier for E2E.
  • − 13 px body text is small for occasional users, and the inspector needs a 375 px plan (a bottom sheet).
  • − The violet accent is a common "AI tool" look.

All three studies: opaque fixed and floating surfaces (alpha 1, no backdrop-filter); visible :focus-visible; motion reduced to 150 ms opacity under prefers-reduced-motion; every text pair at or above WCAG AA in both themes (the tables are in each study's header). Google Fonts load only to preview the studies. The app self-hosts the same families through next/font. The post previews are neutral placeholders, not real posts.
