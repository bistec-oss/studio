Instrument — a dense, tonal workbench

The post is the work object; the UI is the bench around it. Cool true-neutral greys, one ultraviolet accent, a canvas stage with an inspector, hairlines everywhere and tonal steps instead of big shadows. Built for people who spend the day in it.

Palette

True neutrals (zinc-like, hue-free). Accent: ultraviolet, used only for the primary action, the current version, the active nav rule and focus. Exported shares the accent hue — "ready" reads as "next action".

canvas surf-1 surf-2 fg muted line accent draft sched publ failed subtle

Type

Geist 400–600 for everything human-readable; Geist Mono for labels, breadcrumbs, readouts, versions and times — the machine layer. Scale 10.5 · 11.5 · 12.5 · 13 · 15 · 18.

next/font/google: Geist, Geist_Mono → --font-sans, --font-mono

Surface & elevation

  • Tonal: canvas → surface-1 (panels, chrome) → surface-2 (raised). Dark goes lighter as it rises.
  • Hairline line-subtle dividers; controls carry a 3:1 line edge.
  • Menus/toasts/FAB: opaque + a tight 1 px ring shadow. No blur, no backdrop-filter.

Density

Compact. 4 px grid; controls 30 px (24 small); body 13 px / 1.45; nav rows 30 px.

Radius

4 · 6 · 8 px. Controls 6, floating surfaces 8. No pills.

Motion

Fast: 90 / 140 / 200 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1); ≤ 2 px travel, press = 1 px down. Reduced: 150 ms opacity.

Focus

2 px accent outline, 1 px offset (shown on the refine input).
