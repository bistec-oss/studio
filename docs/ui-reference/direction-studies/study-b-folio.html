Folio.

Editorial and flat. A warm paper ground, an ink-and-sienna palette, a serif voice for headings and a quiet grotesk for the work. Structure comes from rules and typography, not from boxes and blur — the post sits on a proof plate like a page waiting for sign-off.

01Palette

Paper (cream, hue ≈ 40°) and espresso ink. One accent: burnt sienna, used for the primary action, the current-page rule and focus. Status hues are muted, printed-ink versions.

02Type

Fraunces (optical size 9–144) for titles, section heads and the refine prompt; Instrument Sans 400–600 for UI; JetBrains Mono for code only. Scale 11 · 12 · 13.5 · 15 · 18 · 24 · 42.

next/font/google: Fraunces, Instrument_Sans, JetBrains_Mono → --font-display, --font-sans, --font-mono

03Surface & elevation

  • Flat. Panels are areas between rules; no cards, no blur shadows.
  • Floating surfaces (menu, toast, FAB) are opaque surface-2 with a 1 px ink border and a hard 4–6 px offset shadow.
  • No backdrop-filter anywhere.

04Density

Medium, reading-first. 8 px grid on a 24/26 px baseline; body 15 px / 1.6; controls 36 px (30 small). Generous column gutters (56 px).

05Radius

2 · 4 · 6 px. Nearly square — rules and type do the softening.

06Motion

Quiet: 120 / 180 / 240 ms on cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1); fades plus a 3 px drop, never scale. FAB presses into its shadow. Reduced motion: 150 ms opacity only.

07Focus

2 px sienna outline, 2 px offset (shown on the refine prompt). ≥ 6.2:1 on paper, ≥ 6.6:1 on espresso.
