- **Why:** T5's notes require the desktop and mobile sidebars to pass AC-09, but the aside used `.glass-panel`. That class is in-flow and stays translucent until its screen migrates (FR-07, design §5), so the `.glass` / `.glass-popover` source fix could not reach it.

- **Ruling:** the orchestrator accepted it as the smallest change that meets the task's own AC. The alternative was `test.fixme` on two AC-09 checks until T6.

## What changed, by file

**`src/app/globals.css`**

- **Surface classes.** A new `@layer components` block holds `.surface`, `.surface-raised` and `.surface-overlay`, per DESIGN_SYSTEM §4.1:
  - `.surface`: surface-1, a line-subtle border, radius md, the panel shadow.
  - `.surface-raised`: surface-2, a 1 px fg border, radius md, the raised shadow.
  - `.surface-overlay`: surface-2, a 1 px fg border, radius lg, the overlay shadow.
  - All three set `background-color`, never the shorthand, and none has a `backdrop-filter` or an alpha.
- **`.glass` is now opaque:** surface-1 with a line-subtle border, no blur; its dark rule is removed.
- **`.glass-popover` is now opaque:** surface-2, a fg border and the raised shadow; its dark rule is removed.
- **Unchanged:** `.glass-panel` and `.glass-input` (FR-07).
- **New `@keyframes drop` and `.animate-drop`:** a 3 px drop over `--dur-base` with `--ease-standard`.
- **New `[data-sonner-toast][data-expanded='false'][data-front='false'] > * { opacity: 0 }`.** Sonner's `unstyled` mode drops its own version of this rule.
- **Reduced motion:** `.surface-raised`, `.glass-popover` and `[data-sonner-toaster] [data-sonner-toast]` get `animation: fade 150ms linear both !important`. The toast selector has two attributes on purpose, so it outranks Sonner's own `animation: none !important`.

**Primitives (`src/components/ui/*`)**

- **Button.**
  - **Variants:** the names are kept, and a new `ink` variant is added.
    - `primary`: accent fill.
    - `secondary`: the Folio Outline button.
    - `ghost`: the Folio Text button, underlined.
    - `danger`: the outline shape in `status-failed`.
    - `ink`: fg fill with canvas text.
  - **Sizes:** sm 30, md 36, lg 40 px.
  - **Base:** `font-text font-semibold rounded-ui-md whitespace-nowrap`, plus the focus ring `focus-visible:ring-2 ring-focus ring-offset-2 ring-offset-canvas`.
- **Panel.tsx (new):** `cn('surface', className)`.
- **Input.tsx (new):** exports `fieldClasses`, `fieldEdge(error)`, `fieldLabelClasses` and `fieldErrorClasses`.
  - The field: surface-2, radius sm, padding 10 by 14 px, `text-ui-base`.
  - The edge is `line`, or `status-failed` on error.
  - Focus: `focus:border-focus focus:ring-1 focus:ring-focus`.
- **Select:** the same field styling. It is a native `<select>`, so it has no menu content.
- **SegmentedToggle:** a `line` outline. The active segment is `bg-fg text-canvas`, and the focus ring is inset. The roles and `aria-selected` are unchanged.
- **StatusChip:** no `dark:` variants. Each chip is `bg-status-x/10 text-status-x` in both themes, with an inset 1 px `currentColor` ring. It is 22 px tall, `text-ui-2xs`, uppercase via CSS only.
- **Modal and Drawer:**
  - The scrim is `bg-scrim/50`, and the Drawer's blur is removed.
  - The content is `.surface-overlay`, and `animate-modal-in` is kept for the centring.
  - The title is `font-display text-ui-xl`.
- **ConfirmDialog:** token text; the buttons, labels and roles are unchanged.
- **ImageLightbox:**
  - The scrim is `bg-scrim/80` with no blur. The content uses `animate-drop`, no longer `animate-scale-in`.
  - The close button and the caption are `.surface-overlay`.
  - Download is `<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">`, with the same name.
  - The image is shown as rendered.
- **QueryError:** uses Panel and token colours.
- **index.ts:** exports `Panel`, `Input` and the aliases `GlassPanel = Panel`, `GlassInput = Input`.
- **Shim and alias:** both. `GlassPanel.tsx` and `GlassInput.tsx` are one-line re-export shims, because about 30 screens import those paths directly. T13 deletes them.

**`src/components/providers/ToastProvider.tsx`**

- Sonner runs `unstyled: true`. The toast is `surface-overlay rounded-ui-md`, per DESIGN_SYSTEM §4.1; the title is display, the body muted, and the icon coloured per type.
- The close button is positioned explicitly, and the width is 340 px.
- T1's offsets are kept. `useTheme` and `theme` are dropped, since the tokens switch on their own.

**AC-10 check:** `ui/*` and `ToastProvider` were grepped for the guard patterns, `dark:`, `black`, `white`, `primary` and hex colours. The only hits are the `primary` variant name and `whitespace-nowrap`. `tsc` is clean.
