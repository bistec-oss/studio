## Changes by file

- **`brief/page.tsx`**
  - **Head:** the Folio page head ("Create" eyebrow, "New Brief" display title, muted lead), left-aligned at `max-w-3xl`.
  - **Action bar:** under a 2 px `fg` rule. Back is Text; Continue and Generate Post are the view's one primary.
  - **Behaviour untouched:** `disabled`, `stepValid`, `copyProviderReady` and `handleGenerate`.
- **`cardCls.ts`**
  - **`cardCls(selected)` option cards:** a `border-line` edge, `hover:border-fg`, and when selected a `bg-surface-raised` fill with a 2 px ink frame. Focus is the §9 outline. No glass and no blue.
  - **New `rowCls(selected)` ruled rows:** selected gets `bg-surface-raised` and the 2 px accent bar (§8.6).
  - **Also exports** `FOCUS`, `SMALL_CAPS`, `STEP_NUMERAL`, `STEP_HEAD`, `STEP_LEAD` and `NOTICE`.
- **`StepHead.tsx` (new):** an `aria-hidden` italic accent numeral, then a display h2 (unchanged text), then a muted lead.
- **`Stepper.tsx`**
  - **Layout:** an `<ol>` of ruled columns. The top rule is accent for the current step, `fg` for done steps and `line-subtle` for steps ahead. Under it sit an italic numeral (an accent check once done) and the label in small caps.
  - **Semantics, additions only:**
    - `aria-current="step"`.
    - Below sm the label is `sr-only` rather than `hidden`. A done step used to have an empty name on mobile; at sm and up the names are unchanged.
  - **Behaviour unchanged.**
  - **One deliberate deviation:** the done check uses `strokeWidth={2}`, against §8.16's 1.4, because at 1.4 it was too faint.
- **`FieldLabel.tsx`:** small caps on tokens; still a `<label>` with no `htmlFor`, so no names change.
- **`CampaignStep.tsx` / `CampaignRow.tsx`:**
  - **Picker:** ruled rows (`rowCls`) under a 2 px `fg` rule, with small-caps group heads.
  - **Kit banner:** a `.surface` line.
  - **Briefing preview:** a ruled disclosure, now with `aria-expanded`. Its version number moves from mono to muted sans.
  - **Removed:** the decorative dot and dashed square, which had no text.
- **`SizeDesignStep.tsx`:**
  - **Layout:** groups separated by `line-subtle` rules. The size cards are `grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-3` (three columns at 375 would clip "1080×1920 px").
  - **Card titles:** in display.
  - **States:** `text-status-failed` errors and `.surface` empty states.
- **`TemplateCard.tsx`:** `cardCls` plus a `line-subtle` hairline around the swatch. The swatch keeps its inline `style={{ background: template.previewColor }}` from data, marked `// ui-exception:` (`TemplateCard.tsx:27`).
- **`ContentStep.tsx`:**
  - **Fields:** `fieldClasses` + `fieldEdge()`. The placeholders, `autoFocus` and `maxLength` are unchanged.
  - **Enhance view:** "Before" is a `.surface` block and "AI suggestion" is ink-bordered paper.
  - **Scroll regions:** both scroll containers are `tabIndex=0 role="region"` labelled "Before" / "AI suggestion", with inset focus (the T7 review rule).
- **`ImagesStep.tsx`:**
  - **Rows:** uploads are ruled rows, and the user's image is never restyled.
  - **Controls:**
    - Embed / Style ref is a small outline toggle; it was blue or violet before.
    - Remove is a quiet icon, with its aria-label unchanged.
    - Add image is a dashed `border-line` button.
  - **The amber note** becomes a muted ruled aside. It is help, not a warning (§3.2).
- **`ReviewStep.tsx` / `ReviewRow.tsx`:**
  - **Structure:** see "ReviewStep extensibility" below.
  - **Notices on status tints:** no copy provider is `status-scheduled` (the amber warning), an error is `status-failed`, and generating is `status-exported`.
