## Concerns and notes for T3/T4

1. **I added `--line-subtle` to the required token list.** NFR-01 needs 3:1 for real boundaries, such as input and control edges and the focus ring. A 3:1 `--line` used for every decorative divider would look heavy. So `--line` is the 3:1 boundary token, and `--line-subtle` is for decorative hairlines only, which WCAG 1.4.11 exempts. T4's contrast test should declare `line/*` at 3:1 and leave `line-subtle` out. B adds `--font-display`, and every study adds `--scrim` and `--shadow-panel`.
2. **The reduced-motion approach in design §4 has a side effect.** Setting `transition-duration: 1ms !important` on `*` also turns on a 1 ms transition for every property, because `transition-property` defaults to `all`. A computed-style read taken right after a state change can therefore catch an intermediate value; I saw a 3 px outline for 1 ms. It is harmless on screen, but T5's AC-18 and AC-17 E2E should settle briefly before they read computed styles, or the rule could be scoped.
3. **Status hues.** None of the directions uses blue, so `scheduled` moves from today's sky blue to amber/ochre. Green stays only as the semantic `published`/"applied" colour, never as identity. In B the accent (sienna) and `scheduled` (ochre) are in the same warm family. In C, `exported` deliberately shares the accent. Both choices are flagged in the index trade-offs.
4. **What the studies change, for the user to judge** (studies are not binding on FR-12):
   - All three move Export and Edit inline into a More menu, and add an Undo button by the versions.
   - B drops the nav icons.
   - C is a three-pane layout. That is the largest restyle (T9), and it needs a 375 px plan for the inspector (FR-10, A4).
   - Any FR-12 accessible names stay as today when the pick is applied.
5. **The placeholder post previews use literal greys.** They stand in for rendered content, which is the documented post-preview exception. They are not tokens.
6. **The Artifact hand-off is not done.** tasks.md says to publish the comparison as an Artifact and stop for the pick, and I left that to the caller. `index.html` loads the three studies as sibling files, so a publish needs them passed in `files`. Each study also works standalone.
7. **Scratch helpers are left in the scratchpad, outside the repo:** `contrast.mjs` (it computes the ratios and rewrites the tables between `<!-- CONTRAST:START/END -->`), `shoot.mjs` and `behave.mjs`. `contrast.mjs` is a ready reference for T4's `contrast.test.ts`.
