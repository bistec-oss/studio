## 1. Legacy layer removed (FR-13)

- **`globals.css`:**
  - Deleted: the Frozen Light `:root` / `.dark` var blocks; `.glass`, `.glass-panel`, `.glass-popover` and `.glass-input` (with their variants); `.glow-blob`; and the unused `scaleIn` / `.animate-scale-in`.
  - Updated:
    - `.glass-popover` is out of the reduced-motion selector;
    - the body is now `rgb(var(--canvas))` / `rgb(var(--fg))` / `var(--font-sans)`;
    - `::selection` and the scrollbar thumbs are on tokens, so their `.dark` variants are gone.
- **`tailwind.config.ts`:** removed the `light-*`, `dark-*` and `primary*` colours and `spacing.appbar` / `spacing.sidebar`. `status-*` is flat now (no `dark` sub-key). Tailwind's default size and radius keys are not redefined.
- **Primitives:** `GlassInput.tsx` and `GlassPanel.tsx` are deleted, and `index.ts` drops the aliases. The "formerly Glass…" comments in `Input.tsx` and `Panel.tsx` were removed, because the guard flags them.
- **`AppShell.tsx`:** one stale comment line was removed.

## 4. Guard test (AC-14): `tests/unit/uiTokenGuard.test.ts`

- **What it checks:** it walks `src/**/*.{ts,tsx,css}` with the three design §7 regexes plus `GlassInput|GlassPanel`. Lines containing `ui-exception:` are skipped, which covers both the `//` and `{/* */}` forms. Each hit fails as `file:line: match (label)`.
- **Its three cases:**
  1. the scan is non-trivial (over 100 files, including `globals.css`);
  2. there are zero hits across `src/`;
  3. a self-test that every pattern fires and both exception forms are honoured.
- **Result:** zero hits across `src/`. This is also the AC-12 grep over the whole tree.
- **Mutation proof:** appending `"glass-panel bg-light-surface text-slate-500"` to `QueryError.tsx` gave 3 hits at `:35`. The file was restored and the diff is empty.
- **Also updated: `tests/unit/designTokens.test.ts`.** Its FR-07 checks now assert the legacy vars and colours are **absent**, the `status-*.dark` mappings are dropped, and `fontFamily.sans` is expected to be `var(--font-sans)`.
- **Unit count:** 1724 became 1722, from 5 `.dark` mapping cases removed and 3 guard cases added.
