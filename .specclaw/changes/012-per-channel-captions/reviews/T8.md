## Findings

### [NOTE] src/app/api/generate/copy/route.ts:36-45 - behaviour change from 422 to 200 on an unusable reply

**Problem:** before T8, an unusable Instagram field gave 422 `COPY_ERROR`. Now a fully rejected reply returns 200 with every caption `FAILED` and `headline: null`. This follows FR-10's per-field shape and is disclosed in the report, but a pre-012 caller that only checks `res.ok` will treat an all-failed reply as success.
**Fix:** none required. Confirm the only in-repo caller (wizard / enhance) reads `captions[].status`; T11+ panels are the likely place.

### [NOTE] src/mcp/tools/generate.ts:90-96 - caption read after a paid generation

**Problem:** in `generatePost`, `captionList` runs after `generateDraftForBrief`. If the loader throws, the caller gets an error although the draft exists.
**Fix:** acceptable; the draft id is recoverable and the loader is a plain read. No change needed.

### [NOTE] src/mcp/tools/generate.ts:116-135 - second read of the draft in `getDraft`

**Problem:** `getDraft` does `findUnique` and then `loadDraftCaptions` (which reads the draft again for headline/legacy state). One extra query per call.
**Fix:** none; the team guard before the loader is the reason, and it is correct.
