## AC-24 and the T12 review's risk R1

**R1 is confirmed not to be a bug.** The risk was that the review notice clears only once the panel's `caption` prop refreshes after the blur-save. TC-CAP-19 never reloads or navigates after the edit. It:

1. fills IG;
2. blurs it, which saves through the PATCH, after which `onSaved` makes the page call `fetchDraft()` again;
3. asserts that the notices drop from 2 to 1 and that IG's description loses the notice;
4. checks that LI keeps its notice.

So the refreshed prop does reach the panel, and only IG's notice goes. No refetch fix was needed.
