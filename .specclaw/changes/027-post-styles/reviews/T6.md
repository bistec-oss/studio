## Findings

### [NOTE] src/components/brief/useBriefWizard.ts (resume block, ~line 405) - Resume

Resume computes touched-ness with `campaign: null` (campaigns load later). If a stored tone equals the style's own tone while the saved campaign has a different `defaultTone`, the field is "untouched" and, once the campaigns query resolves, the tone shown can change from what the user saw. Rare, no crash, and the report discloses the opposite case. Optionally recompute when campaigns load; not needed for T6.

### [NOTE] src/components/brief/useBriefWizard.ts selectCampaign (~line 515)

Selecting a campaign overwrites a style the user already picked on step 1 (campaign is step 0, so order normally prevents this; going Back and re-picking a campaign would reset it). Matches FR-07 wording ("selecting a campaign replaces..."), just worth knowing. Touched tone/image style are never overwritten, as required.

### [NOTE] src/components/brief/PostStylePicker.tsx - Mobile length

Eight full-width cards stack to ~900 px at 390 px (screenshot step1-picker-dark-390), pushing size and design far down. Usable, accessible, no horizontal scroll. A two-column grid at narrow widths or shorter summaries would help; optional.

### [NOTE] src/components/campaigns/ScheduledQueueSection.tsx:~208 - Held line

`max-w-[16rem]` makes the amber sentence wrap to 5-6 lines in the status column (screenshot queue-held-light-1440). Readable and the title carries the full text, but it makes held rows tall. `max-w-[16rem]` is an arbitrary value; uiTokenGuard passes. Could be widened.

### [NOTE] src/components/drafts/FactsCheckNotice.tsx

Correct: `Notice tone="warning" role="status"`, values only (mono code) plus place label, no caption or brief text, Publish untouched. The repeated value per place ("$49 (headline), $49 (Instagram caption), ...") is noisy at 390 px but accurate.
