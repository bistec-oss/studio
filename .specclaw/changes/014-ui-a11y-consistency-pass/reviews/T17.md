### [NOTE] tests/e2e/a11y.test.ts (AC-16 `expectStaticRegion`)

`region.focus()` plus `toBeFocused()` proves that `tabindex=0` is focusable. It does not prove that a keyboard Tab reaches the region. This is falsifiable: removing `tabIndex` fails it. A Tab-order walk would be stronger, but is optional.
