## A regression found, and the orchestrator's fix

**The regression:** T3 had added `min-w-0` to PageHead's actions wrapper. That let the Add user and Add team buttons overflow their column by up to about 22 px at 768–864 px on `/admin/users` and `/admin/teams`.

**The fix (orchestrator):**

- The wrapper is back to `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-2`.
- A new `actionsClassName` prop is added, and only the library passes `"min-w-0"`.
- Updated: `PageHead.tsx`, `library/page.tsx`, `uiPrimitives.test.ts`, the design §2 contract and DESIGN_SYSTEM §8.18.

**The re-sweep, 640–1440 px in 2 px steps, read-only:**

- No page-head action overflows on users, teams, brand kits, campaigns, projects or the library.
- The library search width equals the pre-T3 layout: 253.17 px at 768, 270.48 px at 800, and 288 px from 860.
- `main`'s ARIA snapshot is unchanged at 375, 768 and 1440.
