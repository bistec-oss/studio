### [NOTE] tests/unit/uiTokenGuard.test.ts — pattern 5 (positive tracking)

**Problem:** `/tracking-\[0?\.\d+em\]|letter-?spacing\s*:\s*['"]?0?\.\d+em/i` catches only sub-1 em literals written in `em`. `letterSpacing: '1em'`, `tracking-[1em]` and `rem` values slip through. The pre-T8 pattern had the same limit, no real src hit exists, and Folio used 0.1–0.16em. Optional hardening: `\d*\.?\d+r?em`, but it must keep passing the negative `-0.01em` sample.
