## Design choices and deviations

- `compositionNoticeFor(stamp)` takes only the stamp (design said `(stamp, label)`); the label is derived inside from `styleLabel`.
- The rejected refine render carries the same stamp a commit would (`commitStamp`, not `prev`): if the refine also replaced the background, "Use anyway" would otherwise adopt a render with a hero stamp that no longer describes it. Differs from design section 8 text ("carries prev").
- Refine contract is still passed when the same refine replaces the background (spec says so; the stamp then becomes classic).
- E2E gotcha: a refine without a sentinel in its instruction reads `__MOCK_BG__` from the brief topic, so a "carry" refine on a `__MOCK_BG__` draft needs `__MOCK_BG_NOT_NEEDED__` in the instruction.
