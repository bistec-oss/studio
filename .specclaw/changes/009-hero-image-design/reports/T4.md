## Budget: worst cases against 900 s (margin 60 s, so a segment must be at or under 840 s)

New step bounds: `heroCheckMs` = `HERO_CHECK_TIMEOUT_MS` (20 s), `layoutMeasureMs` = `LAYOUT_OBSERVE_TIMEOUT_MS` (15 s).

| Segment                                              | Before    | Now (worst mode)                     | Headroom |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ | -------- |
| Async generation: captions saved to pre-design touch | 210 s     | **230 s** (api)                      | 670 s    |
| Async generation: pre-design touch to finalize       | 820 s     | **835 s** (api); CLI-only 715 s      | 65 s     |
| Regenerate design: claim to pre-design touch         | 210 s     | **230 s** (api)                      | 670 s    |
| Regenerate design: pre-design touch to settle        | 820 s     | **835 s** (api)                      | 65 s     |
| Scheduled generation (both rows), MCP, refine, copy  | unchanged | unchanged (scheduler last row 830 s) |          |

`actionBudgetViolations(CATALOGUE, STEP_BUDGETS)` returns `[]`. The design's first sizing (layoutMeasureMs = 60 s + 30 s) was tried and **failed the gate**: the API-mode design segments reached 910 s, over the limit once the margin is kept. That is why the bound below was chosen.

## Carry-overs from the T3 review

1. **Seams drive `check-error` and `region-invalid` through the real planner and `evaluateRegions`** (`heroSeams.test.ts`). `check-error`: `__MOCK_HERO_CHECK_ERROR__` makes the seam throw, `checkHero` settles it, and the real `planComposition` and `settleComposition` produce the stamp. `region-invalid`: no fifth sentinel was added (the design lists four); instead `planComposition` is given an injected archetype map whose region needs `minAreaPct: 90`, the seam's calm samples cover 45%, and the real `evaluateRegions` returns `region-invalid`. The sampler-mock tests also cover null, a too-small share and too few samples.
2. **`observeLayout` is bounded: I chose the wrapper and sized the budget to it.** `observeLayout` now races the measurement against `LAYOUT_OBSERVE_TIMEOUT_MS` (15 s, an optional 4th argument for tests); on expiry it logs and returns `null`, so nothing is recorded. The budget uses that constant. I did not size the budget to `networkidle0`'s 60 s cap because that fails the unit gate (above). Known limit: the abandoned page keeps its renderer slot until setContent's own 60 s cap; a real measurement takes about 1 to 2 s.
